Conference Venue and Transportation

Forum 2026 will be held at Mong Man Wai Building (MMW) LT1&2, the Chinese University of Hong Kong (CUHK), Hong Kong, China.

In-campus shuttle bus service for speakers will be provided.

Transportation

The Chinese University of Hong Kong (CUHK) is located in New Territories (新界), Hong Kong. The university entrance is directly connected to Exit A of University Station (大學站) on the MTR East Rail Line (港鐵東鐵線).

During the forum, shuttle bus services will be arranged to connect the university entrance, the forum venue, and the dining areas. Please proceed to the designated pick-up points according to the relevant notices.

Accommodation

For easier transportation, accommodation near the MTR East Rail Line, such as Sha Tin (沙田), Tai Po Market (大埔墟), University (大學), and Fo Tan (火炭) is recommended. When making a reservation, it is advisable to use apps or platforms such as Trip, Agoda, or Booking.com to compare options.

Friendly Reminders

  • To enter the university campus, please present a photo ID, such as a passport, Mainland Travel Permit for Hong Kong and Macao (往來港澳通行證), or Hong Kong Identity Card.
  • Smoking is prohibited throughout the university campus. Please take note.
  • The MTR accepts WeChat Pay, Alipay, and Octopus Card. Taxis generally accept Hong Kong dollar cash only.
  • For more related information please refer to: https://www.cuhk.edu.hk/english/university/visitors.html
