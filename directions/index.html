Conference Venue

Forum 2026 will be held at Mong Man Wai Building (MMW) LT1&2, the Chinese University of Hong Kong (CUHK), Hong Kong, China.

Forum shuttle bus directions:

5 June

  • 08:10 & 08:30: Shuttle buses to the conference venue: pick up at CUHK MTR Station Exit A
  • 12:15–13:45 (Lunch): Shuttle buses between conference venue and restaurant: pick up near conference venue
  • 17:45–21:45 (Dinner): Shuttle buses between conference venue and restaurant: pick up near conference venue

6 June

  • 08:25 & 08:45: Shuttle buses to the conference venue: pick up at CUHK MTR Station Exit A
  • 12:30–13:45 (Lunch): Shuttle bus between conference venue and restaurant: pick up near conference venue
  • 18:15: Shuttle bus from venue to CUHK MTR Station: pick up near conference venue

Transportation

The Chinese University of Hong Kong (CUHK) is located in New Territories (新界), Hong Kong. The university entrance is directly connected to Exit A of University Station (大學站) on the MTR East Rail Line (港鐵東鐵線).

Suggested transportation method: take MTR to University Station (大學站) enter the campus (registration at campus entrance is needed), then take our Forum shuttle bus (please take note of our pickup times)

Other methods:

  • Take taxi to University Station (大學站), enter the campus (registration at campus entrance is needed), then take our Forum shuttle bus (please take note of our pickup times)
  • Directly take taxi to Forum venue (registration at campus entrance is still needed).

In the morning of 5 and 6 June, 2026, shuttle bus services will be arranged to pick up speakers from the university entrance to the Forum venue. Two-way transportation from the Forum venue to the lunch (5 and 6 June, 2026) and dinner (5 June, 2026) places will be provided.

Accommodation

Accommodation needs to be self-arranged.

For easier MTR transportation, it is suggested to book the hotel near the MTR East Rail Line, such as Sha Tin (沙田), Tai Po Market (大埔墟), University (大學), and Fo Tan (火炭). When making a reservation, it is advisable to use official booking apps or platforms such as Trip, Agoda, or Booking.com to compare options.

Important Reminders

  • To enter the university campus, please present a photo ID, such as a passport, Mainland Travel Permit for Hong Kong and Macao (往來港澳通行證), Macau ID, or Hong Kong Identity Card.
  • The MTR accepts WeChat Pay, Alipay, and Octopus Card. Taxis generally accept Hong Kong dollar cash only.
  • Smoking is prohibited throughout the university campus.
  • For more related information please refer to: https://www.cuhk.edu.hk/english/university/visitors.html
